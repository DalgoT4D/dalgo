# October 31, 2026

Your notifications now say what they're about before you open them. And Gujarat finally shows up on the district map. It was there all along, just shy.

## Notifications, Sorted by Type

Every notification now carries a label, so you can tell a pipeline alert from a shared dashboard at a glance.

- **Type labels** — each notification shows what it's about: sharing, access, mentions, schema changes, pipelines, platform updates, or announcements.
- **Filter by type** — pick one or more types from the new filter next to the All / Read / Unread tabs to see only what matters to you. Your pick stays put when you switch tabs.
- **Tidier list** — every row is now the same height. Click a row to read the full message, and click again to fold it back.
- **Mark everything at once** — after selecting all notifications on a page, you can extend the selection to every page and mark them all read or unread in one go.

## Gujarat District Maps

- **Drill down into Gujarat** — map charts can now drill down from Gujarat into its 34 districts, including the newer Vav-Tharad district. Use **Download Districts** in the map settings to get the exact district names your data should use.

## Fixes

- **Testing and saving slow sources** — some data sources take a while to check their settings, and testing or saving them could time out before they finished. Dalgo now waits up to two minutes, so slower sources go through.
